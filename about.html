Our Story

Started in a Two-Car Garage

VerdantWood began in 2008 as a single carpenter's workshop, building custom dining tables for neighbors who couldn't find furniture that would actually last. Word spread, and the workshop grew into a full showroom — but the standard never changed.

Today our team spans furniture makers, material sourcers, and interior stylists, all working from the same principle: build it once, build it right.

Design Philosophy

Form Follows How You Actually Live

Three principles guide every piece we design, from first sketch to final finish.

Honest Materials

Solid wood, real stone, and metal — never a printed veneer pretending to be something it.

Considered Proportion

Every dimension is tested against real rooms, not just rendered for a photograph.

Built to Outlast Trends

We design for twenty years of use, not twenty months of style.

Manufacturing Process

From Raw Lumber to Finished Piece

Step 1

Select Lumber

Each board is chosen by hand for grain, density, and moisture content.

Step 2

Shape & Join

Traditional joinery techniques are used wherever the design allows.

Step 3

Hand Finish

Natural oils and waxes are applied in multiple thin coats by hand.

Step 4

Quality Inspection

Every piece is inspected against a 40-point checklist before shipping.

Craftsmanship & Materials

Two Sides of the Same Standard

Our makers and material sourcers work side by side, ensuring every board that enters the workshop is worthy of the hands that shape it.

Our furniture makers train for a minimum of three years before leading a build independently. Every joint is cut and fitted by hand.

We source FSC-certified hardwoods and quarried natural stone, verifying every supplier against our sustainability standards.

Our interior consultants hold formal design credentials and specialize in translating client vision into livable room plans.
