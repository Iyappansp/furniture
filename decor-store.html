Interior Architect Tips

3 Rules for Styling Modern Decor

Master the art of creating harmony, depth, and sculptural interest on mantels, consoles, and coffee tables.

01

Rule of Threes & Asymmetry

Group decor objects in odd numbers (1, 3, or 5) with varying heights to guide the eye vertically and create natural visual rhythm.

02

Tactile Material Contrast

Pair cold polished brass or mirror surfaces with warm organic stoneware, un-dyed linen, or raw travertine for tactile balance.

03

Ambient Layering

Position alabaster lamps or glass pendants near reflective mirrors or gold wall art to double warm light emission during evening hours.
