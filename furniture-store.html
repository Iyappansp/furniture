Interior Architect Principles

3 Rules for Arranging Modern Furniture

Master the balance of spatial proportion, circulation flow, and focal anchoring in open living areas.

01

Circulation & Traffic Paths

Maintain at least 30 inches of walk-through clearance around seating groups and 18 inches between coffee tables and sofas.

02

Material Anchoring

Balance low-slung soft upholstery with raised slim-legged accent chairs and solid hardwood credenzas to maintain optical lightness.

03

Proportional Scale

Match coffee table lengths to approximately two-thirds of your primary sofa length for optimal geometric balance.
