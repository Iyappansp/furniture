Bedroom Design Tips

3 Rules for Designing the Perfect Bedroom

Master the art of creating a serene, restful retreat with proper proportions and material harmony.

01

Prioritize Low-Profile Proportions

Platform beds with lower profiles create an illusion of ceiling height and calm spatial openness.

02

Harmonize Warm Wood Tones

Pair natural walnut nightstands with soft bouclé or linen headboards for tactile balance.

03

Layer Ambient and the Lighting

Place warm dimmable bedside lamps at eye level to ease transition into restorative sleep.
