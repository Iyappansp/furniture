Charred Walnut

Signature Hardwood

Our premium American walnut is sustainably sourced and hand-finished with zero-VOC natural oils. Known for its rich, chocolate tones and organic grain flow, it brings an immediate warmth and sense of luxury to any space.

  • Source: Midwest United States (FSC Certified)
  • Finish: Zero-VOC Satin Matte Oil
  • Best For: Dining Tables, Media Consoles, Accent Shelving

Natural White Oak

Hardwood Finish

Selected for its straight grain pattern and exceptional strength. Our white oak is lightly white-washed and finished in a low-sheen protective seal, preserving its light, airy, and organic Scandinavian aesthetic.

  • Source: Appalachian Forests, USA (Sustainably Farmed)
  • Finish: Low-Sheen Polyurethane Protectant
  • Best For: Bed Frames, Dining Benches, Sideboards

Golden Teak

Outdoor & Accent

Grown in certified plantation forests, our teak wood is naturally high in protective oils, making it resistant to moisture and pests. Left natural, it matures to a silvery-grey, or oiled to keep its warm gold luster.

  • Source: Southeast Asian Plantations (FSC Certified)
  • Finish: Organic Teak Sealer Coating
  • Best For: Patio Dining Sets, Lounge Frames, Entry Benches

Carrara Marble

Tabletop Surface

Sourced directly from Italian quarries. Each slab is selected for its unique, delicate grey veining against a pure white background. Ground and polished by hand, then sealed with a stain-resistant coating.

  • Source: Tuscany, Italy (Premium Quarried)
  • Finish: Stain-Resistant Matte Sealant
  • Best For: Coffee Table Tops, Dining Table Inserts, Console Tops

Brushed Brass

Details & Hardware

Crafted from solid brass and brushed to a warm, soft sheen. Unlacquered to develop a gentle, organic patina over time, or sealed to retain its original golden luster.

  • Source: Recycled Brass, Europe
  • Finish: Hand-brushed Satin Lacquer
  • Best For: Cabinet Hardware, Table Leg Frameworks, Light Details

Fluted Glass

Structural Accent

Architectural-grade tempered glass featuring a beautifully textured, ribbed design. It diffuses light softly to create privacy while allowing a clean, modern play of shadows and reflections.

  • Source: Local Architectural Glassworks
  • Finish: Textured 8mm Tempered Safety Glass
  • Best For: Cabinet Door Fronts, Room Dividers, Light Panels
