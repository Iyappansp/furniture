Dining Design Principles

3 Rules for the Perfect Dining & Kitchen Space

Master the balance of proportion, flow, and focal anchoring in dining and kitchen areas.

01

Table-to-Room Proportion

Allow at least 36 inches of clearance around the dining table for comfortable chair movement, and 42 inches near kitchen passages.

02

Material Harmony

Pair solid wood tables with contrasting seat materials — velvet upholstery against oak, or leather against marble — to create depth.

03

Lighting Centerpiece

Position a pendant light 30–36 inches above the dining table surface for optimal ambiance and visual weight.
